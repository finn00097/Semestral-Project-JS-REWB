Why I love volleyball

Volleyball is a fast team game where nobody can win alone. Every point needs a good reception, a precise set and a strong attack – and that's exactly what I like about it.

Two teams of six players are separated by a net. Each team may touch the ball three times before sending it back. A set is played to 25 points (with a two-point lead) and the match is best of five sets – the deciding fifth set goes only to 15.

Player positions

  • Setter

    The "quarterback" of the team. Touches the ball second and sets it up for the hitters.

  • Outside hitter

    Attacks from the left side, receives serves and plays both in attack and defence. My position.

  • Opposite

    Attacks from the right side and blocks the opponent's outside hitter. Often the top scorer.

  • Middle blocker

    The wall at the net – blocks attacks and hits fast quick sets in the middle.

  • Libero

    Defensive specialist in a different colour jersey. Can't attack above the net or serve in most leagues.

A typical training

15 min

Warm-up

Running, dynamic stretching and jumps to prepare the shoulders and knees.

30 min

Passing & setting

Forearm passes and overhead sets in pairs, then reception of serves.

30 min

Attack & block

Approach steps, spikes from position 4 and blocking at the net.

45 min

Game

6 vs 6 match – the best part of every training.

Basic volleyball rules

  1. Every rally wins a point (rally point system).
  2. A team may touch the ball at most 3 times before it crosses the net (a block doesn't count).
  3. One player can't touch the ball twice in a row (except after a block).
  4. Players rotate clockwise every time their team wins back the serve.
  5. Touching the net or stepping over the centre line is a fault.
  6. Back-row players can't attack the ball above the net in front of the 3 m line.
