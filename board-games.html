Friday game nights

Every Friday evening my friends and I meet around a big table. Someone brings snacks, someone brings a new game and we play until late at night. Modern board games are much more than Monopoly – there are deep strategy games, cooperative games where everyone wins or loses together and fast party games for big groups.

The full list of games I own or played is in my Game Library.

My favourite board games

Catan

Trade, build roads and settle an island. A classic that started my board game hobby.

3–4 60–120 min Strategy
Ticket to Ride

Collect train cards and claim railway routes across the map. Easy to teach.

2–5 30–60 min Family
Codenames

Two teams, one-word clues and a lot of laughing. The best party game.

2–8 15 min Party
Pandemic

Work together to stop four diseases spreading around the world.

2–4 45 min Cooperative
Azul

Draft beautiful tiles and decorate the walls of a royal palace.

2–4 30–45 min Abstract
Wingspan

Attract birds to your wildlife preserves and build a strong engine.

1–5 40–70 min Engine building

Types of board games

Long-term planning, resource management and a bit of luck. Examples: Catan, Terraforming Mars, Brass: Birmingham.

All players are one team against the game. Examples: Pandemic, The Crew, Gloomhaven.

Quick rules, many players and lots of fun. Examples: Codenames, Dixit, Activity.

Players build the board from tiles during the game. Examples: Carcassonne, Kingdomino, Cascadia.

Game night rules

  • The host chooses the first game, the winner chooses the next one.
  • New players always get the rules explained – no one is left behind.
  • No greasy snacks at the table – cards must stay clean!
  • Phones in pockets while playing.
  • Everybody helps to put the game back in the box.
