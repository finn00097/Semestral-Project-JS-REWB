Why I go to the gym

I started going to the gym to jump higher and hit harder in volleyball. After a few months it became a hobby on its own. Lifting weights clears my head after school and seeing progress every week is very motivating.

I train three times a week using a push / pull / legs split and add one mobility session for recovery.

Tips for beginners

  • Technique first. Learn the movement with light weight before adding kilograms.
  • Warm up. 5–10 minutes of cardio and a few lighter sets.
  • Progressive overload. Add a little weight or one more rep every week.
  • Sleep and eat. Muscles grow when you rest, not when you train.
  • Be consistent. Three trainings every week beat six trainings once a month.
