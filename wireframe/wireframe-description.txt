SEMESTRAL PROJECT - WIREFRAME OF THE HOMEPAGE (MOBILE VIEW)
Author: Jiří Špičák, semester project 7REWB
Image:  wireframe-mobile.png (also wireframe-mobile.svg)
Screen: typical smartphone, 375 px wide - all content is in one column

Structure of the homepage from top to bottom
(the numbers match the orange numbers in the wireframe image):

1. HEADER
   Dark navigation bar that stays at the top while scrolling (sticky).
   On the left the logo - styled text "SEMESTRAL PROJECT" with a lightning icon and my name.
   On the right a hamburger button. On a phone the 7 menu items
   (Home, Volleyball, Board Games, Video Games, Gym, Game Library, Join Me)
   are hidden and open as a drop-down list after a click.

2. CAROUSEL
   Full-width slideshow right under the menu with 4 rotating images
   (volleyball, board games, video games, gym). Every slide has a box with
   a title and a button that leads to the page of that hobby.
   Arrows on the sides and indicators at the bottom switch the slides,
   the slides also change automatically every 5 seconds.

3. BREADCRUMB NAVIGATION
   Shows where the visitor is ("Home"). On the other pages it shows the path,
   e.g. Home / Hobbies / Volleyball.

4. INTRO
   Round avatar with my initials, heading "Hi, I'm Jiří", a short text about the
   website and three buttons in different styles:
   "More about me" opens a MODAL window with details about me,
   "My game library" leads to the table, "Join me" is a link-style button.

5. MY HOBBIES
   4 cards (Volleyball, Board Games, Video Games, Gym). On a phone they are
   below each other (1 per row), on a tablet 2 and on a PC 4 in a row.
   Each card has an image, a round icon, a title, a short text and a
   "Read more" button.

6. STATISTICS
   4 small boxes with a number and a short description in a 2 x 2 grid
   (on a PC all 4 in one row).

7. TABS "MY WEEK"
   4 tabs: Schedule, Goals, Playing now and FAQ. Only the content of the active
   tab is visible; clicking another tab shows its content
   (a table, progress bars, text and an accordion).

8. CALL TO ACTION
   Dark box with the text "Want to play together?" and a button that leads
   to the Join Me page with the form.

9. FOOTER
   Logo, short text, round social media icons, a list of links to all pages,
   contact information and the copyright line.

10. BACK TO TOP BUTTON
    Round floating button in the bottom right corner. It appears after scrolling
    down and smoothly scrolls the page back to the top.

Hidden elements (not visible in the wireframe until opened):
   - MODAL window "About me" (opened by the button in part 4)
   - drop-down menu (opened by the hamburger button in part 1)
